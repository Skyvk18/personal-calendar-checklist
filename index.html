How Daybook Works

01
Today (Lock In)

Your main page automatically pulls today's tasks and timed events from your calendars so you can focus and check them off.

02
Calendar & Day Agenda

Switch to Calendar and click any day to open its full timeline ("At 4:00 PM — ..."), add items, or edit existing ones.

03
Multiple Calendars or Combined

Create labeled calendars (e.g., School, Work, Fitness) using + in the sidebar. View them all combined or focus on one at a time.

04
Planner Chat

Type naturally like "Today there is tennis practice at 5pm" or "Tomorrow at 3pm Dentist" and it extracts only the relevant title, date, and time onto your calendar.
